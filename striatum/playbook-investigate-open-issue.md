# Playbook: Investigate Open Issue

## Objective
To thoroughly review and assess an open issue.

## Steps
1. Retrieve the issue details (title, author, content).
2. Analyze the request for clarity, feasibility, and alignment with project goals.
3. Determine confidence level (starts at 0.3 for new voices).
4. Record observations in cortex/ for future reference.
5. Decide on appropriate action (respond, implement, defer, close).
