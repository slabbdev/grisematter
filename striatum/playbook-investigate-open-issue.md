  **Playbook: Investigate Open Issue**

  **Goal:** Understand the content of an open issue and determine appropriate action.

  **Steps:**
  1. Retrieve the issue details (title, description, author, comments).
  2. Analyze the request for clarity and feasibility.
  3. Consider the request against existing memory and project goals.
  4. Formulate a response or plan of action.
  5. Record findings and decision in the hippocampus log.
